# Product

QueueVeld — Make every service appointment count.
AI appointment capacity planning for hospitals, outpatient clinics and diagnostic centres.
Founder: Ayomide Akinola. Co-founder: Emmanuel Nwafor.
Features: demand forecasting, waiting-time estimation, cancellation patterns, capacity recommendations, admin dashboards.
MVP: history imports, demand forecasts, scheduling dashboards. AWS: SageMaker AI, Bedrock, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3.
Model: monthly subscription by facility size. Main action: Request a pilot. Never clinical decisions.
