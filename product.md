# QueueVeld — product
Make every service appointment count.
AI appointment capacity planning for hospitals, outpatient clinics and diagnostic centres:
forecast patient arrivals, estimate waiting time, read cancellation patterns, and plan clinic capacity.
- Founder/CEO: Ayomide Akinola · Co-founder/CTO: Emmanuel Nwafor
- Buyer: clinic operations managers, outpatient directors, diagnostic centre leads.
- Main action: Request a pilot.
- MVP: appointment history imports, demand forecasts, scheduling dashboards.
- AWS (planned): SageMaker AI, Bedrock, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3.
- Model: monthly subscription by facility size.
- Never claims clinical decision-making; forecasts are planning aids reviewed by staff.
