The eight-week clinic pilot

  1. History import and baseline, weeks 1 and 2

    You send twelve months of appointment exports. Names and record numbers are removed before anything is stored.

  2. Forecasts beside your process, weeks 3 to 8

    Each Monday at 06:00 a new 14-day forecast arrives, and we measure it against what really happened.

  3. Weekly reports for administrators

    Managers accept, change or decline each room suggestion, and every decision is logged.

The forecasting platform on AWS

The pilot platform we are building. The public demo calls none of these services.

  • S3 and Lambda

    Encrypted imports, identifiers removed row by row

  • SageMaker AI

  • Bedrock

    Plain-language weekly summaries that quote their figures

  • DynamoDB

    Services, rooms, suggestions and the decision log

Questions

What clinic managers ask before a pilot.

Ask about a pilot

What data do we need?

Twelve months of bookings as CSV or an EHR export: booking date, appointment time, service, room and status. No clinical notes.

Does it change our bookings?

No. QueueVeld reads exports and has no write access to your scheduling system.

What does it cost?

A monthly subscription priced by facility size: one clinic, a hospital department or a network of sites. We quote after the pilot.

How accurate is the forecast?

We measure it on your own past weeks during the pilot and show the error band on every chart.

Where is our data kept?

In an AWS account of its own, in the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit.

Can we stop the pilot?

Yes, at any time. We delete your data and confirm it in writing.
