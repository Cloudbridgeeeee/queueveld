# Design
Concept: "the clinic day board" — the hour-by-hour rhythm of a waiting room.
Colour: mist #EEF2F4 (ground), ink #14233B (text), saffron #F2A93B (ticket / capacity), coral #E2553A (overflow), sage #5F9283 (on-track), white cards.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, tight, large), Figtree (body). No mono labels, no all-caps eyebrows.
Signature: hero arrival curve that draws itself against a capacity line; hours above capacity fill coral.
Motion: one hero sequence; reveals restrained; hover lift on interactive cards only; reduced-motion safe.
Radius: 18px panels, 999px pills for ticket chips. Avoid gradient washes and purple.
